## First, what "Dior lip gloss" usually refers to

Dior's lip gloss line has largely collapsed into two families:

- **Dior Addict Lip Maximizer** — the plumping gloss. Minty, slightly thicker, and the one people mean when they say "the one that tingles."
- **Dior Addict Lip Glow Oil** — the shiny one. A lip oil with a gloss finish, reformulated recently into three distinct finishes.

Both come in 6 mL / 0.2 oz tubes, both are built on cherry oil, and both sit in the same price bracket in the US. Dior also releases seasonal sets and limited editions that come and go, so anything you see labelled "holiday" may not exist by the time you decide.

Confusing the two is the single most common mistake. They are not interchangeable, and the difference isn't nuance — it's texture, finish and how much colour you get.

## Dior Addict Lip Glow Oil: the reformulated shiner

This is the one that got a visible overhaul. The current version comes in three finishes, and knowing which you're buying matters more than the shade number:

- **Juicy** — glass-like shine with a transparent tint
- **Glaze** — noticeably more pigment, soft to medium colour payoff
- **Sparkly** — micro-glitter, glittery finish

Dior claims the formula adjusts to your lips' pH to reveal a "custom" colour, and cites 24 hours of hydration plus 8 hours of smoothing from instrumental tests on 30 subjects. Across the range, colours like 000 Universal Clear, 001 Pink, 004 Coral, 006 Berry, 007 Raspberry, 010 Holo Pink, 012 Rosewood, 015 Cherry, 081 Latte Glaze, 104 Black Cherry Glaze and 088 Mocha are commonly stocked.

The texture change is the real story. Beauty reviewers who compared old and new versions describe the reformulated oil as slightly thinner and more comfortable, still cushiony, still intensely shiny, but easier to wear — and more hydrating than the original. Others note that the pH-reactive colour mostly stays close to what the tube shows, and that the Sparkly and Juicy finishes pull sheer while Glaze delivers actual pigment.

## Which one should you buy

The decision doesn't need to be agonised over:

- **You want shine and colour, and hate tingle** → Addict Lip Glow Oil, Glaze finish if you want actual pigment, Juicy if you don't.
- **You want the minty plumping feel and a slightly thicker cushion** → Addict Lip Maximizer in a high-shine finish.
- **You want to layer over lipstick** → Lip Maximizer, applied after. This is the use Dior itself suggests for the volume effect, and it's how most people get visible results.
- **You buy once and want no regrets** → stay on the permanent shades. 001 Pink and 012 Rosewood are the low-risk picks across both formulas.
- **You're chasing maximum savings** → verify the exclusion list first. A 3% rate at a merchant that includes Dior beats a 20% rate at a merchant that doesn't.
